Judgement calls worth challenging:

- **Targets come from the biz team** — DPD 0 at 98%, DPD 1-30 at 55%, DPD 31-90 at 13% (`TARGETS`). Total Mitra and DPD 90+ carry none: the first is an aggregate of the others, and nobody is held to a number on the oldest bucket. Both are left uncoloured rather than given an invented verdict.
- **A missed bucket reports a shortfall in mitra, not percentage points.** "kurang 37 mitra" is something a BP can act on; a percentage-point gap is arithmetic the reader has to convert first.
- **The recommended action keys off the bucket with the biggest shortfall in mitra**, because the three failures want different responses: nothing collected at DPD 0 is what a surprise visit tests, DPD 1-30 is a coaching problem, and 31-90 is past what a BP can fix alone. A BP clearing every standard gets no action at all — one on every row would bury the ones that need it.
- **A created task lives in the module store, not the grid**, because switching tab or state unmounts the grid and a task disappearing mid-walkthrough is worse than useless.
- The **Flow rate to DPD 1-30** metric scores downwards while Repayment rate scores upwards; `Metric.higherIsBetter` is what keeps one of them from reading backwards.
- `DisbursementTable` / `DisbursementMetrics` (`disbursement-table.tsx`) — the Pencairan tab, which used to be a "belum dirancang" placeholder. NoA and Nilai each split into mitra baru / mitra lanjutan, plus the rupiah still missing from the month's target.
- **Renewal is shown as a rate, not just a count.** Eleven renewals is excellent on a book with twelve mitra maturing and poor on one with twenty, and the target ("85% renewal mitra cair per bulan") is written as a percentage — so the mitra lanjutan cell carries "% dari N" under its count, against a per-BP `renewalDue`.
- **Pencairan is reported by the MONTH, Pembayaran by the week.** The targets were always monthly; the page used to report a week and pace them, which meant a figure was judged against a quarter of the number printed beside it. The scope line above the table now follows the tab.
- **Pencairan wears colour on one figure only — the renewal rate.** Same rule as Pembayaran: counts and rupiah are facts and stay black, the rate is the judgement. The earlier red/orange/green banding coloured almost every cell, which left nothing standing out, and it is gone along with the standalone "Kurang dari target" column — the two shortfalls now read as caption lines beneath their own group, the way Pembayaran does it.
- **"With Leads monitoring"** (`disbursement-table-leads.tsx`, `store.pencairanVariant`) is a second Pencairan cut, picked from the STATES panel same as Pembayaran's mvp/end, and now the dashboard's own default (`DEFAULT_TAB` in `data.ts` is `'disbursement'`, and `pencairanVariant` starts on `'leads'`) — a BM opens this dashboard to check disbursement against the month's target first. The three headline cards drop their % and "Target: …" label — read as plain counts and rupiah. **Potential mitra is its own group, never folded into Mitra baru** — both in the panel above the table and as its own column group inside it — because a lead is not yet a mitra: Leads tanpa KTP, Leads dengan KTP, Follow up, UK (uji kelayakan) and Disetujui (`leadsUnqualified`/`leadsQualified`/`leadsFollowUp`/`leadsUk`/`leadsDisetujui` on each `DisbursementBp`, `potentialMitraFunnel()` in `data.ts` — field names kept from an earlier "Unqualified/Qualified" pass; only the on-screen labels changed, to name what the stage actually is instead of a status a BM had to go look up). Mitra baru keeps its plain NoA/Pencairan, unchanged from the default cut. The panel adds one more box past Disetujui, behind a chevron: labelled "Disbursed" (the same figure as Mitra baru's NoA), highlighted in primary — the funnel's actual target, not just its last stage. **Potential mitra isn't just closed by default — it isn't rendered at all until asked for.** The entry point is a ghost button in Mitra baru's own card ("Lihat potential mitra", `BucketCard`'s `footer` slot), not a header bar that sits on the page whether or not anyone wants it. Once open, the panel is bonded to the card that opened it by an accent line — the card's bottom edge, a short connector bar, the panel's top edge — rather than a full border around the card: a full border around only Mitra baru, next to two cards still in `border-default`, read as "this one is selected" (a filter-chip's grammar), not "this one continues below"; the edge-only version was the fix. Narrowing the panel to fit under just Mitra baru's column was also considered and dropped — it stays full width with the same spacious grid the rest of the cut uses. **Disetujui and NoA are kept as separate figures, not merged**: an approved lead can still miss disbursing in the period it was approved in (paperwork, the mitra backing out, a slot slipping to next month), so Disetujui can run ahead of NoA — collapsing the two into one column was tried and reverted because it hides exactly the gap this cut exists to surface. The shortfall caption sits under Mitra baru's own NoA, since that's the figure the target is judged against; Potential mitra carries no shortfall of its own, being a leading indicator rather than a pass/fail line. The table's own control is a gsheet-style entry point: a slim gutter column (`GUTTER_COL_WIDTH`) sitting between Total and Mitra baru, holding a pill handle with no border or fill of its own, so it reads as a seam in one continuous table rather than a separate lane — driven by the same `potentialOpen` state as the ghost button, so the two can't drift out of sync.
- **Option B of the Leads cut** (`disbursement-table-leads-b.tsx`, `store.pencairanVariant` `'leadsB'`, "With Leads monitoring: Option B" in the STATES panel) sits beside the original, which stays as it is. **In B the branch funnel panel is gone; the funnel is read in the table at both levels.** The original draws the branch's stages as a panel bonded under Mitra baru's card, behind a "Lihat potential mitra" ghost button — the same numbers as the table, in a second shape, with a second control to keep in sync. Now: **Potential mitra is a permanent `Lead` column** sitting between Total and Mitra baru, always carrying each BP's lead total, **and the `Lead` column label is itself the control** — a chevron beside it, pointing right to open the five stages and left to fold them back. The gsheet-style pill in its own gutter column is gone, along with the gutter: the lead total is the collapsed reading of those five stages, so the thing that opens them belongs on that label rather than on a separate handle floating between two columns, and the table loses a 48px lane that carried nothing. The group is titled **Leads**, and **every group header is now a single line** — the "Target …" subtitles came off Total, Mitra baru and Mitra lanjutan too. The targets are still on screen where they're acted on: the headline cards carry them as captions, and the per-BP shortfalls still read under the columns they judge. The branch's own figures are now a **`Total cabang` summary row pinned at the top of the table body** (`BranchRow`, `bg-neutral-50`, bold), carrying every column including the funnel's — so a BM compares a BP to the branch by looking straight up the column rather than back at a panel drawn in a different shape. The headline cards keep their three buckets, unchanged.
- **Option B: opening the stages widens the table, never the columns already on it.** The table is `table-layout: fixed` with every data column pinned in `COL_WIDTH`; Nama is fixed too, and the window's slack goes to an empty spacer column at the far right — letting Nama absorb it was tried first and is exactly the bug it looks like: Nama shrank the moment the table outgrew the window. Column widths are budgeted to the widest string each one ever holds (Pencairan carries the branch line's `Rp1.270.000.000` in bold, so it is 200px, not the 116 that clipped it), and to roughly fill a full-width window when closed, so the spacer is a sliver rather than a dead gap. Without this the browser re-solved every column when the five stage columns appeared, and a figure a BM was reading slid sideways mid-comparison. Header cells are one fixed `h-20` line box (`HEAD_LINE`, `whitespace-nowrap`) in both rows and every state, so the chevron beside Total can't make its row taller than its neighbours. The `Total` label is the disclosure and is the only coloured thing in the header — `primary-500`, the action colour, since it is the one clickable cell there. The **Total cabang** row is tinted `primary-50` rather than `neutral-50`: it's the branch's own line, not another BP with a zebra stripe.
- **The detailed breakdown is its own state, "Pencairan: NTB detail" (`pencairan-leads-detail`), not a rewrite of "With Leads monitoring".** Both read `DisbursementBp`'s per-BP lead counts, but the flat cut (`leadsFollowUp()`/`leadsUk()` helpers in `data.ts`, collapsing the stored halves back into one count) and the detailed cut (`leadsFollowUpInterested`/`leadsFollowUpNotInterested`/`leadsUkDraft`/`leadsUkSubmitted` read directly) are picked with a single `detailed` boolean threaded through `DisbursementTableLeads` → `DisbursementMetricsLeads`/`BpRow`, rather than two separate files — the table chrome, the gutter toggle, Total/Mitra baru/Mitra lanjutan are identical either way, so only the Potential mitra group's shape (`potentialGroup(detailed)`, 5 cols flat vs 7 detailed) and the funnel panel's boxes (`FUNNEL_STAGES_FLAT` vs `FUNNEL_GROUPS`) branch on it. A flat "Follow up: 2" told a BM nothing actionable — a KTP problem, an interest problem and a paperwork problem all look identical as one number; the detailed cut's New mitra (Dengan KTP / Tanpa KTP), Follow up (Interested / Tidak interested) and UK (Draft / Submitted) point at different fixes instead. `FunnelBox` in the panel takes an optional `parts` list and prints them under the stage total rather than beside it. Disetujui and the final box (Dicairkan in the detailed cut, Disbursed in the flat one — the flat cut's original name, left alone) stay flat single-value boxes in both cuts — nothing left to break either of them down by. "NTB" in the state's name is the same shorthand `ngmis-bm-monitoring-v2`'s daily task types already use for new-mitra acquisition work (`ntb-fu`, `ntb-mitra` in that project's `daily-data.ts`), not a name invented for this screen. **The detailed cut's table header carries a third tier**, matching how the panel above already groups the seven leaf columns into three boxes: New mitra / Follow up / UK each span their own pair of leaf columns (`POTENTIAL_SUBGROUPS`), Disetujui rowSpans straight through since it has no breakdown. Every other column — Total's, Mitra baru's, Mitra lanjutan's — grows a `rowSpan={2}` to span past this extra row instead of repeating its own label there; `showSubgroups` (`detailed && potentialOpen`) is the one flag that decides whether that middle row renders at all, so the flat cut keeps its plain two-row header untouched.
