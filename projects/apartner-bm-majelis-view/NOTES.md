# Notes — apartner-bm-majelis-view

Forked from `apartner-majelis-view` (A-Partner BP New Concept). What differs:
the handset belongs to a Branch Manager (Nurhayati), every majelis carries the
Business Partner who runs it, both directories filter on BP, and the day is two
briefings instead of the BP's field stops.

## Project-local components (§4)

- `BpLine` / `BUSINESS_PARTNERS` (`bp.tsx`) — the Business Partner as data and as one caption line directly under the majelis or mitra name. Authored once and attached to the majelis; a mitra inherits hers from her group, so the two directories cannot disagree about who owns her. It was a grey pill; a badge is for something that changes, and whose book a group is on is a standing fact that read as a third status among the real ones. Used on the majelis card, the mitra card and the mitra page.
- `MajelisCard` (`majelis-card.tsx`) — the one majelis card, shared by the Majelis directory and the group's own page (where it replaced a one-line address strip). It was the directory's private row; lifted out so the BP line is drawn once. `onOpen` is what separates the two uses — tappable row in a list, plain header block on the page.
- `AgendaCard` / `AgendaSteps` / `CheckMark` / `BriefingCard` + `MORNING_AGENDA` (`briefing.tsx`) — the briefing's running order as data, and the pieces the page draws it with. `AgendaSteps` renders an item's numbered script — a flat list, or, where the item carries `groups`, one sub-headed list per scope ("Keseluruhan cabang", then "Per BP") — shared by the Alt-1 card and the Alt-2 stepper page so the two never drift; `topRule` drops the leading divider on a stepper card that has nothing above the steps (repayment / disbursement, which no longer print an NG-MIS subtitle). `AgendaCard` makes the WHOLE card the tap target with the mark on the left: the BM is talking to seven people while she uses it, and a checkbox sized for a cursor is not something you hit mid-sentence. `CheckMark` is ONE size everywhere (24px) with a solid `neutral-400` ring while unticked — the old dashed `border-default` hairline was too faint to read as a control at all — and a filled primary disc once done. Used on `briefing-morning.tsx`; the evening page will take the same three.
- `briefing-live.tsx` (`BookSections` + `PeriodsCard` / `BookTaskCard` / `TaskActionSheet` / `PointerBlock` / `BpTugasSections` / `contributionsFor` + `REPAYMENT` / `DISBURSEMENT` / `BP_TASKS`) — the in-app data behind Alt-3/4/5 of the morning briefing. Alt-1/Alt-2 print the NG-MIS path and stop; the in-app cuts draw the numbers she would open NG-MIS for on the handset itself. A book (`BookSections`) is a stack of per-BP SECTIONS: the branch total ("Total Branch Ciseeng") first, then the seven BPs, each a section TITLE — her name, with a "N dari 8" place-in-the-run counter pinned quiet at the right — over her progress card (`PeriodsCard`). The card leads each of its two periods with the one number she acts on, the amount still to collect ("Tambah Rp… lagi", or "Target tercapai"), over a meter, with the target read quietly under it — the capaian percentage is gone, the same move the KPI page makes. Whether the stops ride in the book is the Alt-3 / Alt-4 fork, on `BookSections`' `withTasks`: **Alt-3** turns it off — the books show only numbers and the day's stops stay a tugas step of their own (`BpTugasSections`, every BP on one page, all her stops as cards, no book figure); **Alt-4** turns it on — each stop is a `BookTaskCard` under the BP it belongs to, filed by the book it moves. Every stop carries the slice of today's target it brings where it has one (`contributionsFor` — repayment takes MV/HV, disbursement takes MV/Sos/FU; a sosialisasi or follow-up lands no figure, because growth doesn't settle the same day), printed beside its name, plus a 3-dot menu (`TaskActionSheet`: tambah ke tugas saya / jadwalkan ulang). **Alt-5** swaps the book body for `PointerBlock` — the achievement and the stops read out as bulleted pointer sentences at reading size ("Bulan ini, target …, masih kurang …" / "MV Majelis Mawar (07.30), tagih …"). Dummy and small (a branch total + seven BPs, a day of stops each). The morning briefing switches between the cuts on `store.morningVariant`; the closing photo is an inline card on Alt-1 and a bottom sheet over the last step on the stepper cuts.
- `BriefingRow` (`today.tsx`) — the BM's task card, drawn as the BP app's `TaskBody`: a two-letter code tile (MB / EB), a caption carrying the appointment and its clock with the status pinned right, the name at reading size, the Amartha Point, then distance as a pill. The kind and the name are the same two words here, which they are not on a BP's card.

- `BannerTag` — a pill for a tag sitting ON the inbox detail's coloured hero; `Badge`'s tints all assume a light ground. Used in `banner-detail.tsx`. Ported from `apartner-homepage-ia`.
- `TabBar` — the three L0 destinations (Jadwal / Majelis / KPI), hidden inside a pelayanan. Ported from `apartner-task-first`.
- `AgendaRow`, `HeaderAction` — the schedule's clock gutter and its top-bar icon buttons. Ported from `apartner-task-first`.
- `StageBar` — numbered stages with rails; a cleared stage goes grey and swaps its number for a tick, an upcoming one is a ring around its number, and only the stage she is standing in is coloured. One coloured thing on the bar, because stating where she is is the only job it has — green ticks behind her competed with it and made a four-step bar look like three results and a question. The one purple rail runs OUT of the active stage, toward the one she is heading for. Used on attendance, collection, growth, recap and all three home-visit steps.
- `WeekStrip` — horizontal repayment rail carrying the amount inside each week. Superseded on payment pages by `WeekGrid` + `AngsuranCard`; kept for any surface that still wants the scrollable, amount-per-week form.
- `WeekGrid` — the cycle as a six-cell band on a lightest-grey fill, paged as a snap carousel (six weeks per swipe, chunked from the newest so any short page is the oldest and pads left; opens on the current week; no stepper or dots): each cell stacks the week's date, its outcome as a status circle (green paid / orange part-paid / red missed / hollow primary ring for the open week), and the rupiah received that week. No dividers and no outer border — the grey ground and the spacing are the only edges — so it reads as one calendar band, flat enough to sit in a card or straight on the page. The amount under each cell is the point: a red Rp0 and an orange Rp100rb are a miss and a shortfall, and only one is a sentence the BP can say. Full history is behind "Lihat Semua".
- `AngsuranCard` — `WeekGrid` + the last instalment that landed + the tagihan breakdown, headed "Riwayat angsuran" with a "Lihat semua →" link into the full ledger. Only the total is bold — the tunggakan and weekly lines are set regular so the sum has something to be louder than — and it is ruled off from the two parts it is made of. `flat` strips the border and padding but KEEPS the heading: a section with no edge needs its name more, not less. "Pembayaran terakhir: …" sits as one quiet line under the grid rather than a card of its own — it answers the question the grid raises, and a mitra whose recent weeks are all empty is exactly the one whose answer is off the left edge of the strip. The one payment card the mitra page, home visit and Tagihan page all open on, so the bill is drawn one way everywhere. Lives in `mitra-card.tsx` beside `TagihanBreakdown`.
- `ResultRow` — what a stage card says once its outcome is RECORDED, as opposed to `ActionRow`, which is right while the card still asks for something. One tinted band per the reference: a mark, what happened and the figure, all in the status colour (green-600 / orange-700 / red-500 on the matching 50-tint), with an outline `Ubah` at its edge. It replaced a caption-over-figure with a badge and a round pencil beside it — three objects saying one thing, where the news itself was the smallest of them; the tint IS the status now, so the badge is gone, and "Ubah" is a word again because a bare pencil on a coloured ground reads as decoration. Whatever the outcome LEAVES BEHIND — the reason, the janji bayar, what is still short, the screenshot behind a Poket claim — hangs under it in a `NoteBlock`: grey, ruled 2px down its left edge, square where it meets that rule, so an annotation is visibly not a second result. Eight states on the collection stage: belum (`ActionRow`), lunas, bayar sebagian, bayar via Poket (full and short), sudah bayar via Poket (self-serve — no `Ubah`, since none of it was the BP's to correct), ditanggung renteng, tidak bayar, berhenti pinjam.
- `PickRow` — the one radio row this direction uses for a question with a fixed set of answers: who was met at the door, how much was paid. Full-width card, title over an optional description, the mark on the RIGHT where the chevron used to be (these rows used to *go* somewhere on touch, and keeping the target still while the meaning changes is the point); picked = brand border and a `primary-50` fill, with an optional `detail` line reading back what the follow-up captured. Picking and committing are two acts now: a row that fires its own sheet on touch means a mis-tap costs a sheet the BP has to back out of over the bill she was reading, with a mitra still counting notes in front of her. The page's Lanjut is the commitment. Used on the majelis Tagihan page and both home-visit steps.
- `DpdBadge` — her bucket, outlined, as a 7-day RANGE ("DPD 31-37") rather than a day count, because the bucket is what ops acts on and what makes two mitra comparable down a roster of 22 — "DPD 34" beside "DPD 31" invites a distinction that means nothing. Three bands, not two: yellow to 7 days, orange to 37, red beyond. `format="long"` still prints "Menunggak 34 hari" where a sentence is wanted.
- `PenagihanCard` — the collection record on the mitra page, under `AngsuranCard` and only for a mitra with attempts on file (every mitra in DPD; nobody who is lancar). Headline is the SENTENCE a BP says at the door — "Sudah 4 kali ditagih dalam 34 hari" — over the same attempts counted by channel; then the janji bayar, tinted, as the one row facing forward (drawn plainly as "Belum ada janji bayar" when there is none, because four visits and nothing committed is itself the finding); then the attempts, newest first, CLOSED by default behind a "Lihat 4 penagihan" toggle — three entries with quoted notes ran taller than the ledger above them, and a BP says "sudah empat kali" off the summary, opening the log only when the mitra argues with it. Every entry names WHO made it, which is the whole reason it is a list and not a counter: a mitra handed over to another BP otherwise arrives with a DPD number and no story. Data and card live together in `penagihan.tsx`, authored per mitra id — a few representative attempts, not a log.
- `MitraBadges` — her standing facts as a wrapping row under the name on the mitra detail page, built from the SAME badges the majelis visit uses on every mitra card (`ProductBadge` + `DpdBadge`, filled), plus `Badge` for the two exceptions: `keringanan` → "Dapat Keringanan", `predisburse` → "Ready to Disburse" (which replaces the DPD badge, a new mitra having no bucket). It used to draw its own outlined, icon-led `StatusChip` family; that is gone — two drawings of "Modal" and "DPD 34" made a mitra read as a different person on her own page than she did in the queue that opened it. `predisburse` added to the `Mitra` type.
- `ProdukCard` / `LabeledRow` / `PhotoLink` / `CircleButton` / `PinButton` — the mitra page's lower detail cards (Produk chips, Detail with majelis + addresses, Penanggung Jawab). `LabeledRow` is a label-over-value card row with an optional right-pinned control and an under-value link; `CircleButton` the round bordered icon button the reference puts every route pin and WhatsApp in. Local to `mitra.tsx`.
- `ProgressCard` — headline value + denominator over a meter. `showPercent={false}` drops the percentage, which was a third way of saying what the value, the denominator and the bar already said, and let the denominator take the right-hand slot. Only stage 2 still uses it: on Kehadiran and Penawaran the sticky footer already prints the same count directly above the button it gates, so the bar was the screen saying it twice, once at each end. `flat` drops the card chrome for the white header band those stages sit it in. Stage 2 runs on CASH only (`cashCollectedTotal` / `cashBillableTotal`) — the self-serve mitra settled through the app, and a mitra recorded as already paid via Poket settled days ago; neither was ever the BP's to collect, so counting them would start the bar part-full for work she didn't do.
- `StickyBar` — pinned footer holding a summary above its button. Used on all six action screens; the collect page needs the summary inside the sticky region or "sisa setelah ini" scrolls away from the button it qualifies.
- `PinMark` / `WaMark` — the two glyphs drawn one way wherever they appear: WhatsApp is always green, the pin is red as a control and inherited as punctuation in front of an address. `PinMark` now draws the shared `MapPin` instead of the project-local `IconPin` — `icons.tsx` strokes at 1.6 where FunDS strokes at 2, so the pin and the WhatsApp glyph sitting in the same pair of buttons were two different weights. Components rather than a convention, because a convention is what drifts — one screen tints the pin, the next inherits `text-caption`, and the BP is left wondering whether the grey one means something else. The only pins left untinted are the ones inside tinted tiles (`ProofTile`'s "Rekam lokasi", the edit sheet's "Ubah lokasi"), where the container already carries the colour.
- `TagihanBreakdown` / `TagihanCard` — total tagihan and its two parts. Superseded everywhere by `AngsuranCard`, which the mitra page, the collect page and now the home visit all open on; kept for any surface that wants the bill without the week strip.
- `MitraCard` / `DpdBadge` / `MitraPhoto` — the one member card, shared by roster, attendance, collection and growth. `MitraPhoto` replaced the initials `Avatar` on every MITRA surface (roster, collect, doorstep): a BP in a room of 22 women recognises a face, and "SH" is something she has to decode into a name and then match to a person. It is a silhouette placeholder — the prototype has no photos — but it says what the real thing is. Leads keep `Avatar`: a prospect genuinely has no photo on file.
- `ActionRow` — the bottom half of a stage card: caption + bold figure left, one control right. Used by all three visit stages, which is what keeps the card one object as the BP moves through them.
- `ChoicePill` — the two-outcome pill pair (Tidak/Hadir, Tidak/Tertarik), selected in primary. Replaces `AttendancePill`'s green/red selection: the pills record what was said, and a red "Tidak hadir" beside a red DPD badge puts two alarms in one colour. Used on attendance, growth, and both steps of the collect sheet. `hideLabel` drops the printed caption where the sheet TITLE already says what is being picked ("Alasan Kurang Bayar" over a list captioned "Alasan kurang bayar" is the same sentence twice).
- `OptionCard` — a radio card that HOLDS its own follow-up (the amount field, a reason list). FunDS `SelectableCard` is a `<label>`, so every click inside it re-triggers the radio and pulls focus off whatever is being typed; this is the same look drawn from tokens with only the header row acting as the label. Used on the collect page.
- `ChoiceList` / `ChosenRow` — a single-choice reason picker as full-width rows with a radio mark, collapsing to the answer + "Ubah" once picked. Replaces the chip wrap for absence and decline reasons: reasons are uneven-length sentences, and a chip wrap gives ragged rows and unequal tap targets. Used on attendance and growth.
- `Chip`, `ChipGroup`, `ProofTile`, `StatRows`, `Meter`, `Avatar`, `IconTile`, `Collapsible`, `Overline`, `SectionTitle`, `VisitTitle` — carried over from `apartner-task-first`.
- `LeadRow` / `LeadIdentityCard` — a prospect as a list row and as the identity block on her own record. Both lead with the interest grade, because a list of leads has no other ranking.
- `ContactButton` — the round WhatsApp/route/handset button, lifted out of `home-card.tsx` into `ui.tsx`: reaching someone is now the whole job on two screens, not one. Neutral DISC, tinted GLYPH: a grey circle with a hairline everywhere, and the icon keeps its channel colour (WhatsApp green, route pin red, handset primary). It used to be tinted whole — a green disc on a green ring, a red one, a purple one — which put three saturated circles on the doorstep card and five down the mitra page, competing with content that has real colour to spend (a red DPD bucket, a green paid week). `mitra.tsx`'s local `CircleButton` matches.
- `KumpulanCard` — the balai's address over the Ketua Majelis' name, with a route and a WhatsApp button on the right: one card, because they are one thought ("the place, and the person who is at it"). It sits in the white header band of **Kehadiran**, where it replaced the "Sudah dicatat" progress bar — the footer already prints how many mitra are unrecorded, attached to the button that gate blocks. Lives in `kumpulan-card.tsx` rather than `ui.tsx` because it reads `profile.ts`, which `ui.tsx` must not depend on.
- `RescheduleSheet` — a bottom sheet for moving a task to another day (reason + a new date, tomorrow → next week), reachable from the `link` slot of the top bar on the FIRST page of each flow only: home-brief (HV), sosialisasi (Sos) and follow-up (FU) — Sos/FU gated on a rostered `activeTask`. Kept off the later steps (Tagih, Bukti & Kirim) so the meta-action lives at the one point the BP decides whether to work the task at all, not mid-flow. A sheet, not an inline block or its own screen; the draft resets on open so a cancelled reschedule leaves nothing on the next task. Recorded in `store.reschedules` (keyed by task id, carrying a `count`), surfaced on the schedule as a "Dijadwalkan ulang" section. After the third move it grows a second path — reject the task with an open-text reason (`store.rejects`, gated by `REJECT_AFTER`) — surfaced as a "Ditolak" section. The majelis flow's equivalent is `SkipSheet` (a `Skip` link on the attendance step's top bar, first page only): a photo-with-auto-geotag proof, like the home visit's Bukti & Kirim, recorded in `store.skips` and surfaced as a "Dilewati" section. All are off today's plate: not to-do and not done.
- `SettlementHistorySheet` — the day's cash story in one bottom sheet, opened from the Setoran header (Riwayat, via FunDS `NavigationHeader`'s `link` slot) and the schedule's "Sudah disetor · Lihat" line. Read-only, no footer: a summary trio (Terkumpul / Sudah disetor / Belum disetor) over two breakdowns — what came IN (cash per pelayanan) and what went OUT (each handover, its road, its VA/kode-unik and the tasks it covered). - `AttendanceChoice` — the register's two-cell answer (Tidak hadir / Hadir) as the final UI draws it: 8px-radius cells filling the card's width, coloured by OUTCOME (green present, red absent) rather than by selection, with the unchosen one dimmed once an answer exists. Not `ChoicePill` — a pill is a button you press to make something happen, this pair is a single field with two values — which is why it takes the input radius rather than the pill shape. Used on attendance; `ChoicePill` still serves growth and the collect sheet.
- `ReasonNote` — the absence reason once picked, as a tinted block ruled down its left edge with a `NotePencil` at the far corner: a quotation attached to the mark it explains, not a form row. Replaces `ChosenRow` on the attendance card.
- `IconTile` gained `blue` and `orange` tints for the two NTB task kinds, and `IconMegaphone` / `IconUserPlus` were added to `icons.tsx`.
- `ProductBadge` — the lending product wherever it appears, on a group or on a mitra. One component so the colours cannot drift between screens, which is the only way a colour code is worth having: Modal blue, Hybrid neutral, and GL not printed at all — it is the default product, so the label marks the exception rather than reporting the norm. Drawn as the reference draws it — the product's own `ProductLogo` mark (Modal only; the asset set has no GL mark) beside its name in the product colour, on no ground at all. It was a solid `Badge`, and on a card carrying three chips the solid one won every time when the product is the least changeable fact of the three. On the visit cards it rides at the right edge of the NAME row (`titleBadge`), level with her name, rather than joining the row of things that change. The palette avoids the green/orange/yellow the status badges own. "Hybrid" is the internal word; it prints **GL Modal Mix**, which names both products in it.

Strong promotion candidates: three projects now want a filter chip over a bottom sheet, and two of them have written it independently. `SearchField` uses the shared `MagnifyingGlass` from `@/design-system/icons` rather than a local icon.

The in-card actions (Tagih, Tawarkan) use the DEFAULT size rather than `sm`: `sm` sets 12px type and the attendance pills are 14px, so the same card was read at two sizes from one stage to the next.

## The L0 layer (Jadwal / Majelis / Mitra / KPI / Profil)

Ported from `apartner-task-first` so the two directions share a front door and
what gets compared is the pelayanan itself, not the way in to it. Two routes,
each opening on what it is for:

- **One button starts work.** Only the "Sekarang" card's button clocks in — a
  majelis goes **straight to Kunjungan 1 — Kehadiran**, a home visit to its own
  step 1. A BP sent by the day already knows the group, so the roster would be a
  page between her and the work.
- **Cards open the record.** Tapping a Berikutnya card opens the **Majelis View
  roster** (majelis) or **the mitra page** (home visit). Reading up on what she
  is riding into is something a BP does constantly, and it must not be the same
  gesture as starting a visit she isn't standing at yet.
- **Majelis tab → the roster**, same as a Berikutnya card. Its own "Mulai
  Pelayanan" starts the work from there.
- **Finishing ticks the schedule either way.** A pelayanan started from the
  Majelis tab still closes the day's row for that group (`finishTask` recovers
  the task from the group when no task id was carried in). The work is the same
  work; only the route differed, and leaving the row open would ask the BP to do
  it twice.
- **Home visits** are ported from `apartner-task-first` — same two steps, same
  one-question-with-three-answers ("siapa yang ditemui"), same inline options
  instead of a sheet. What changes here: the doorstep amount is derived from her
  ledger, so the Tagihan card names the three debts it is made of (this week,
  the missed weeks, any shortfall) instead of printing one fused figure.
- **Majelis tab → the roster; Mitra tab → the borrower.** The two look-up
  surfaces answer different questions. "Who is in Kenanga" is a group; "where is
  Ibu Rina" is a person, and a directory of groups cannot take it — the woman
  phoning the BP does not open with which balai she attends. The Mitra card is
  the roster's card with one line added under the name for her majelis, which is
  the only difference the two lists need: on the roster, the group is the page
  you are already on.
- **Profil** is ported from `apartner-homepage-ia` unchanged except for one cut:
  that version carried a KPI card as a second route into the scoreboard, which
  made sense when Profil sat behind an avatar in a header. Here KPI is its own
  tab one thumb away, so the card would be a shortcut to the thing beside it.
- **Prototype edge:** only Majelis Mawar has a real roster — every task and every
  directory row opens Mawar's 22 mitra under the name of the group tapped. The
  Mitra tab handles this differently: the other groups borrow Mawar's LEDGERS,
  so every card carries a real DPD, product and repayment record, but each takes
  a distinct name from a pool of 100. A list whose whole purpose is search cannot
  show the same woman five times — that does not read as a shared fixture, it
  reads as a bug, and it makes the one gesture the screen exists for useless.

## Where this departs from the reference

- **Payment history screen dropped.** The week strip carries the amount per week, so a 50-row table says the same thing one screen further from the conversation. Designer's call.
- **"Sudah bayar via Poket" is an outcome on both flows.** She paid days ago through Poket and the system has not caught up — common enough in the current system that without a row for it the BP either books cash she never received or leaves a woman in the queue owing money she has already handed over. The BP attaches the transaction screenshot; that upload IS the record, and nothing saves without it, because a payment no system has seen is one person's word until there is a picture of it. The AMOUNT is typed, opening on the full bill rather than locked to it: she pays what she has through the app, and a fixed figure would make the BP record money that was never paid just to get the transaction on file. A short Poket payment leaves the same balance a short cash one does, so it asks the same two questions — the reason and the janji bayar — and the collection card badges it *Sebagian via Poket* rather than ticking it green. It banks as **digital, not cash**: `payMode === 'poket'` keeps it out of `cashCollectedTotal`, out of `cashBillableTotal`, and out of the deposit's cash line, so the BP is never asked to hand over money she is not carrying. The stage-2 meter's target therefore DROPS as she records one, which is the honest depiction — that debt turned out to be settled, and it was never going to be cash in her bag.
- **Tanggung renteng is a GL-only payment option, on both flows.** Joint liability is what the G in GL is: on a Modal loan the debt is hers alone, so offering the group as a payer would record a settlement no group ever agreed to. The majelis collect menu grows a "Tanggung renteng" row for a GL mitra — one tap over a read-back of what it closes, no reason and no janji, because the group left no balance behind — and the home visit's Tagih step, which used to show the option to every mitra, now gates it the same way. It writes the money to the ledger and the payer to `payMode`, so the collection card can say **Ditanggung kelompok** instead of drawing it as cash the mitra handed over.
- **"Tidak Bayar" added as a fourth collect option.** The reference's three choices are all payments, which leaves a mitra who hands over nothing unrecordable — she stays in the queue and the queue never reaches zero.
- **Home-visit outcomes extended to five.** The door is a collections visit, so its Tagih step carries two outcomes the majelis collect page doesn't: **Tanggung Renteng** (the group settles her whole bill under joint liability — the "PAR payment" the visit exists to reach; recorded as a full collection, tagged by `payMode === 'tanggung'`) and **Drop Out** (she is leaving the program — a flag with a reason, not a payment or a promise; it retracts both). The Lanjut gate now requires a COMPLETE outcome rather than only that someone was met.
- **The repeated "Total to Collect" block at the top of the collect page dropped.** It is the third showing of the same breakdown in two taps; only the version above the CTA earns its place, because it moves.
- **The reference's numbers do not reconcile.** Its week strip and its outstanding breakdown imply different totals. Everything here derives from one authored ledger, so Rina lands on exactly the reference's arithmetic — this week 200.000 + two missed weeks 400.000 + a 50.000 shortfall = 650.000 — and "minggu ini saja" leaves 450.000, a custom 300.000 leaves 350.000.
- **The offer is settled on the card.** Per the 2026 reference, a stage-3 card carries the sentence to say with its figure in it (*Tawarkan disbursement Rp5.000.000.*), and the fact that triggered it (`Growth.rationale`) — then two equal outline buttons, *Tidak Tertarik* and *Tertarik*. Neither is filled: the app does not put its thumb on a conversation it is not in. Each answer opens one sheet — *Sudah diproses penawarannya?* for a yes (with an optional screenshot when it was filed on the spot), a reason list for a no — so answering never leaves the queue. This replaced a whole `offer` screen, which was right while the answer needed a page of questions and is a round trip out of the room now that it needs one. `Growth.kind` splits the three cases because each rests on a different fact; the renewal's caption is derived from the ledger (`growthStat`) so it cannot drift from the repayment history her mitra page draws from the same weeks.
- **Dewi Lestari sits at week 44 of 50; everyone else is at week 9.** A group takes its loan together, which is right for the collection stages and left the offer stage with no honest renewal case — at week 9 the only thing worth putting to a mitra is a top-up. One member six weeks from the end is what makes "Tawarkan renewal" a real sentence. Her ledger, ladder and loan history all derive from the same `week`, so nothing else needed authoring.
- **MV Bukti Pelayanan follows the home visit's Bukti & Kirim.** One tile — take the photo — with the geotag read back underneath. The "Rekam Lokasi" tile asked the BP to perform a step the phone does anyway, and the stage read-back above it ("Yang sudah dicatat") reviewed a visit she had just finished recording, on a screen whose job is to close it.
- **The roster order ignores payment status.** Nothing ever sorted by it, but the fixture listed `[...ACTIVE, ...PREPAID]`, which had the same effect: every mitra who had already paid sat in a block at the bottom of all three stages. `rosterOrder` interleaves the two by share, so the pre-paid are spread through the queue in a fixed order that never moves as outcomes are recorded — a register is ordered by membership, and a list that regroups itself around who has settled is a different list from the one the BP marked attendance on.
- **The Ketua Majelis leads every roster, badged as such.** The one place order is not pure membership — and not an exception to the rule behind it, which is that the order must not shift under the BP's thumb: the KM is fixed for the cycle, she opens the kumpulan, and she answers for whoever is missing, so finding her by scrolling 22 cards is a hunt the room itself never makes anyone make. `KetuaBadge` is neutral and outlined: a ROLE, in a label row that already spends its colour on the two things that change.
- **Stage 2 gates stage 3.** Lanjut is disabled until every mitra has an outcome on file — any outcome, including "tidak bayar" — with the count of who is left above it, the same shape as the attendance gate. It used to be a warning that could be walked past, which left a queue nobody would come back to once the BP had left the balai.
- **Quick filters on the register AND the collection roster** — Semua / Sudah dicatat / Belum dicatat on stage 1, Semua / Sudah ditagih / Belum ditagih on stage 2, each chip carrying its count. Same question, same shape, only the verb changes. "Who is left" is what both stages are asked constantly, and it was being answered by scrolling 22 cards looking for buttons. A filter, not a sort: the order underneath never moves. `RosterFilter` rather than a row of `Chip`s because it must hold ONE line on a 360px phone — the pills never shrink and never wrap, and the row scrolls sideways instead; `Chip` is built for the reason lists, where a wrapping group of full sentences is right.
- **The stage lists are static.** One roster, one order, on all three stages — answering a card updates the card in place and never moves it. The "Belum/Sudah Diabsen" and "Belum/Sudah Ditagih" sections are gone: they re-sorted the list under the BP's thumb on every tap, and the woman she is standing in front of has to stay where she was.
- **Both Bukti pages end on the same two buttons.** Primary **Selesaikan Tugas** — the visit is done and the day is waiting — and a secondary **Kirim Bukti via WhatsApp**, never gated on the photo, because the receipt is a courtesy to the mitra or the majelis rather than part of the proof the task is submitted with. What differs is only the audience: the majelis sends its GROUP a line per mitra with the total and anyone still unrecorded named at the foot (a group settles together, and a recap only ops can see leaves the women taking the BP's word for it); the home visit sends ONE mitra her own receipt, or the promise she made if nothing was paid.
- **Bukti is the fourth step, not a coda.** It used to sit outside the stage bar — three stages are the work, proof is the paperwork — which showed a BP two taps from finishing a bar that already read as complete, and made a majelis visit's ending a different shape from the home visit's, where Bukti & Kirim has always been a numbered step. Same bar, same white band, `current={4}`.

## The NTB layer (Sosialisasi / Follow Up)

Two new task kinds on the same schedule as the pelayanan, the doorstep and the
deposit — five stops, two jobs. A BP's NTB target and her collection target are
paid out of the same seven KPI parameters and worked in the same eight hours;
giving prospecting its own tab would let it be the thing she gets to if there is
time, which is exactly how it stops happening.

- **Two tiers of capture.** QUICK — nama, WA, sumber, minat, kapan dihubungi
  lagi — is what a woman answers out loud while nine others wait. LENGKAP —
  alamat, majelis tujuan, pinjaman lain — needs her to think, and asking it in a
  crowded warung is how a BP comes back with four leads instead of ten. The
  `0/10` counter counts the quick tier: a name and a number is a lead, the rest
  is homework.
- **Gaps are drawn, not hidden.** Everything the quick tier skipped appears on
  the prospect's record as a named blank with a count. A lead who can't be
  submitted for want of an address is a lead that dies silently.
- **A promise to call becomes a task.** Recording "hubungi lagi besok" puts a
  Follow Up on tomorrow's agenda, made by the same act that recorded the
  promise. Only tomorrow renders — a lead due in October keeps her date on her
  record and nothing appears anywhere, which is the honest depiction rather than
  a limitation: the app is holding October, and October is not a screen.
- **"Did the call land" is asked before minat.** Most follow-ups don't connect,
  and a form that opens on how interested she is makes an unanswered phone look
  like a lead who went cold.
- **"Siap diajukan" is gated on complete data**, and the gate names the gap and
  offers the jump to fill it. That round trip is why the half-finished call
  lives in the store rather than `useState`.
- **A no is a result.** Same rule as "tidak bayar" in the collection flow: a
  refusal with a reason leaves the pipeline as a record ops can count, instead
  of leaking onto someone's spreadsheet.
- **The day was retimed** to fit both — the sosialisasi takes 14.00–15.15, the
  second home visit moved to 15.30 and Majelis Kenanga to 16.30, so the deposit
  still lands inside its 18.00 cut-off.

## The KPI page — one number per card

Reworked toward the reference's flat-rupiah layout, with one rule: **every card
answers "how many more women".** It used to print four figures per parameter —
a percentage, a target count, the current count, and a rupiah motivation line —
and the BP had to subtract two of them to reach the only one she can act on.

- The subtraction is done for her and the result **is** the headline: *Kurangi 3
  mitra lagi* / *Tambah 3 mitra lagi* / *Target tercapai*. The current count is
  gone outright — a number that exists only to be subtracted from another number
  is a number the app should be holding.
- The hero says *Penuhi 4 target lagi* instead of *3 dari 7 tercapai*. Same
  fact, phrased as work remaining. Period → Juni 2026 is the 7/7 all-clear.
- What survives: the target as small print (a BP gets asked the threshold, and
  nobody recites seven), and the bonus as a pill on the name line (it is what
  makes the gap worth closing). The percentage badge and the footer motivation
  strip both went.
- **Not adopted:** the reference's *Tugas ›* deep link per lagging row, and the
  *3/7* score on the KPI tab. The first contradicts this direction's thesis that
  the schedule owns the work; the second adds a number back to save none.

## Open questions

1. **Does the attendance gate pay for itself?** Collection cannot open until all 22 mitra are marked, per the reference. It means the BP passes the room twice. The 15 pre-paid mitra are seeded present so she marks 7 rather than 22, but the gate is the main thing to judge against `apartner-task-first`, which records attendance and payment on one card in one pass.
2. **Page vs sheet for collection.** This direction opens a full page where `apartner-task-first` opens a bottom sheet. The page can afford the week strip; the sheet kept the queue visible behind it. Both prototypes exist to be compared here.
3. **The credit-limit line on the recap is generic.** Stating the relationship without a number is deliberate — a real figure needs a scoring model this prototype doesn't have, and a fabricated one is the kind of number a BP might repeat to a mitra as a promise.
4. **Contact rows on the mitra page do nothing.** WhatsApp and route are affordances only, same as in `apartner-task-first`.
5. **A prospect has no home between her two tasks.** Designer's call: no fourth
   tab, the schedule owns the work. So the sosialisasi task stays on the day as
   a record and its screen doubles as that event's lead list — the only route to
   a prospect who isn't due today. That holds while a BP runs one session a
   week; the moment she carries fifty open leads across six sessions, "which
   sosialisasi did I meet her at?" becomes a real question with no screen behind
   it. The alternative was a Prospek tab, and it stays the obvious fix if this
   ever bites.
6. **Nothing feeds the KPI page.** "Siap diajukan" says it counts toward
   *Pencairan mitra baru*, but the KPI figures are authored per period and don't
   move. Wiring them would mean the scoreboard changes while you demo it, which
   is either the best argument for the flow or a distraction from it — worth a
   decision, not worth guessing.
